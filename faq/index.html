01Where is the center located?+

KK’s Learning Center IV is listed at 707 N Redbud Ln, Colcord, OK 74338. Use the directions link on the Contact page.

02What are the listed hours?+

Oklahoma Human Services lists Monday through Friday, 6:30 AM to 5:30 PM. Saturday and Sunday are closed. Holiday hours may vary.

03What ages are served?+

The state listing includes infants (0–11 months), toddlers (12–23 months), preschool (24–48 months), and school-age children (5 years and older). Confirm availability directly.

04Does the center accept child care subsidies?+

Yes. Oklahoma Human Services lists this center as accepting child care subsidies. Ask the center about your specific eligibility and enrollment process.

05What is the tuition?+

Published tuition rates were not verified. Please call (918) 801-3821 for current rates and policies.

06How do I find out about openings?+
